# 🎨 Sugar Progress Ring Design & Color Concepts

Here are three distinct concepts for calculating the progress ring's colors and formatting its text labels, focusing strictly on your preferences.

---

## 1. Text & Label Layout Concept
To keep it clean, direct, and focus only on **Days (PLR)**, **Teaspoons**, and **Total Grams**:

```
          / \
        (     )
       (   4.0   )  <-- Main Value (Package Load Ratio / Days of Sugar)
        \  DAYS /
         \     /
          \   /
         16.8 tsp   <-- Total Teaspoons of all purchases
         70g total  <-- Total Grams of all purchases
```

---

## 2. Progress Ring Color Ideas

Instead of clinical "Safe/Danger" theorems, here are three intuitive ways to color the ring:

### Idea A: The "Sugar Viscosity" Concept (Liquid Density)
*   **How it works:** Represents how "thick" or "heavy" the sugar load is, mimicking syrup viscosity.
    *   **0.0 to 1.0 Days (Light/Hydrating):** A refreshing **Bright Teal / Clear Seafoam Green** (like water or light coconut water).
    *   **1.0 to 2.0 Days (Thickening):** A rich **Honey Amber / Golden Orange** (representing sweet, concentrated nectar).
    *   **2.0+ Days (Heavy/Syrupy):** A deep **Maple Purple / Dark Blackberry** (representing heavy, slow-metabolizing syrup).
*   **Vibe:** Feels natural and fluid, rather than medical.

### Idea B: The "Sugar Sunset" Concept (Energy Gradient)
*   **How it works:** Uses a smooth, warm sunset gradient that naturally darkens as sugar increases.
    *   **0.0 to 0.8 Days:** Light Peach / Gold (Warm morning sunrise).
    *   **0.8 to 1.5 Days:** Coral Red (Noon sun).
    *   **1.5+ Days:** Sunset Violet / Magenta (Evening dusk).
*   **Vibe:** Premium, modern, and aesthetically pleasing.

### Idea C: The "Interactive Fruit Ripeness" Concept (Familiar Foods)
*   **How it works:** Inspired by the color of ripening fruit.
    *   **Low (0-1 Day):** Lime Green (Crisp, tart, low sugar).
    *   **Medium (1-2 Days):** Banana Yellow / Peach (Sweet, fully ripe).
    *   **High (2+ Days):** Cherry Red (Super sweet, high concentration).
*   **Vibe:** Gamified, friendly, and food-centric.
