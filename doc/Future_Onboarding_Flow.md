# 🚀 Future Onboarding Flow Specification: CutSugar v2.0.0

This document outlines the UX layout and copywriting assets for the next-generation onboarding experience. The copy is designed like static advertisement banners: direct, visually visceral, and punchy.

---

## 🎬 Pre-Onboarding: Splash Screen
*   **Visuals:** Premium dark/light gradient background. Centered animated mascot (`happy` state) fading in, followed by a sleek circular loading indicator.
*   **Tagline:** 
    > **Goodbye Sugar. Hello Health.**

---

## 👤 Screen 1: The Introduction
*   **UI Element:** Minimalist text input.
*   **Copy:**
    > ### "What should we call you?"
    > *Please enter your name to personalize your health dashboard.*

---

## 🎯 Screen 2: Goal Selection
*   **UI Element:** 4 high-fidelity interactive tiles (multi-select or single select).
*   **Copy:**
    > ### "What is your primary health goal?"
    > 
    > *   ⚡ **Boost Energy & Alertness**  
    >     *Prevent insulin spikes and beat the afternoon crash.*
    > *   ⚖️ **Manage Weight & Cravings**  
    >     *Break the sugar loop and regain control of your appetite.*
    > *   🧠 **Sharpen Mental Focus**  
    >     *Clear the brain fog and maintain sustained mental clarity.*
    > *   🛡️ **Protect Long-Term Health**  
    >     *Align with WHO limits to protect cardiovascular and metabolic health.*

---

## 🧱 Screen 3: Create Your Own Pantry (Advertising Banner Style)
*   **Visual asset suggestion:** Illustration of an elegant, modern home pantry shelf filling with scanned items.
*   **Headline:** 
    > ## Your Personal Sweet-Safe Haven.
*   **Subheadline:** 
    > **Build a curated pantry of low and no-sugar favorites. Scan, collect, and keep track of clean-label items that treat your body right.**
*   **Direct & Punchy Callout:**
    > **"No more guessing at the shelf. Save your go-to clean products in your personal pantry for effortless shopping."**

---

## 🔄 Screen 4: Food Swap (Advertising Banner Style)
*   **Visual asset suggestion:** Side-by-side card showing a red "High Sugar" product morphing into a green "Low Sugar" alternative with a curved arrow.
*   **Headline:** 
    > ## Meet Your Healthy Upgrades.
*   **Subheadline:** 
    > **Food Swap is here. Instantly discover cleaner, lower-sugar alternatives to high-sugar foods the moment you scan them.**
*   **Direct & Punchy Callout:**
    > **"Don't just scan it—swap it. Make better choices instantly with smart, delicious replacements."**

---

## 🥄 Screen 5: WHO Teaspoons Standard (Advertising Banner Style)
*   **Visual asset suggestion:** A literal teaspoon filling up with sugar alongside a percentage indicator.
*   **Headline:** 
    > ## Sugar. Measured in Teaspoons.
*   **Subheadline:** 
    > **Grams and ounces are confusing on purpose. We translate complex laboratory metrics into clear, visual teaspoons—fully aligned with the World Health Organization guidelines.**
*   **Direct & Punchy Callout:**
    > **"Because you don't eat in laboratory units. See your daily limit in real spoons."**

---

## 🛒 Screen 6: Clean Grocery Shopping (Advertising Banner Style)
*   **Visual asset suggestion:** A shopper scanning a barcode with their phone, highlighting the instant swap and save animation.
*   **Headline:** 
    > ## Grocery Shopping, Cleaned Up.
*   **Subheadline:** 
    > **Scan barcodes, build your low-sugar pantry, and use Food Swap to bypass sugary traps on the fly. Smarter choices made simple.**
*   **Direct & Punchy Callout:**
    > **"Your ultimate companion for sugar-free grocery trips. Point, scan, and shop clean."**

---

## 🔍 Screen 7: Hidden Sugar Finder (Advertising Banner Style)
*   **Visual asset suggestion:** A magnifying glass exposing the word "Sugar" hiding behind chemical synonyms like Maltodextrin, Dextrose, and High Fructose Corn Syrup.
*   **Headline:** 
    > ## Expose the Stealth Sugars.
*   **Subheadline:** 
    > **Brands hide sugar under 60+ chemical aliases. Our Stealth Sugar Audit exposes hidden sweeteners instantly, even in products claiming to be 'Sugar-Free'.**
*   **Direct & Punchy Callout:**
    > **"No stealth ingredients. Expose hidden sweeteners disguised as healthy additives."**

---
