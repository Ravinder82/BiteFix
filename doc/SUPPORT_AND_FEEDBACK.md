### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

#### Q: How do I restore my active subscription on a new device?
Open **BiteFix Settings** -> tap **Restore Purchases**. Ensure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

#### Q: How do I cancel my subscription?
Open **BiteFix Settings** -> tap **Manage Subscription**. This will open Apple's native Subscription Manager where you can turn off auto-renewal.

#### Q: Why is a barcode not recognized?
BiteFix queries open food databases containing millions of products. If a barcode is unlisted or missing full ingredient labels, feel free to report the barcode number to us via email so we can queue it for database inclusion.
